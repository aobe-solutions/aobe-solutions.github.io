Proof of Value: The Kanban Board in Action

📍 Real Order Scenario: Customer calls with an event date (Oct 8–10). Follow-up email received with wording. Proof sent and approved. Delivery promised by Oct 8.

Old Way: The Single-Task Scroll (~12 min)

  1. Create single task. Type voicemail notes into comments.
  2. When wording arrives, copy/paste email text into comments with EFC VERB.
  3. Change category dropdown from 20 options to "NSO - VERBIAGE RECEIVED".
  4. Email layout proof; paste graphic into comments with SLO.
  5. Change category dropdown to "SENT LO".
  6. Task goes overdue; manually push due date out — erasing the Oct 8 delivery date.
  7. Type (DEL 10/8) into task title and calendar event so the deadline isn't lost.

New Way: The Kanban Drag (~25 sec)

  1. Create Order: Click "Add Opportunity" under Customer; enter Target Delivery Date: Oct 8.
  2. Send Proof: Click "Save to Insightly" in Outlook sidebar to link proof to the order.
  3. Drag to Layout Proofing: Card automatically schedules a 3-day follow-up task.
  4. Client Approves: Drag card directly to Production (or Permit Filing). Fabrication checklist is created instantly.
  5. Delivery Date (Oct 8) stays permanently locked in place; zero false overdue alarms.
